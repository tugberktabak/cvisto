Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

The short version: CVisto does not require an account. Your resumes, cover letters, photos and personal details are stored on your device. Your resume text leaves your device only when you use an AI writing feature, and only after you have given explicit permission. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CVisto ("the App", "we", "us") handles, how it is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can control or delete it. CVisto is developed and operated by Tuğberk Tabak, an independent developer based in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the processing described here.

1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

To build your resumes and cover letters, you may enter information such as your name, job title, email address, phone number, address, links, work experience, education, skills, languages, certificates, a profile photo and the text of your cover letters.

All of this content is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers and we cannot see it. This also applies to:

  • Resume import: PDF files, scanned paper resumes (camera) and LinkedIn data export files that you choose to import are read and processed on your device.
  • Profile photos: photos you select from your library or take with the camera are processed (for example cropped or background-adjusted) on your device.
  • ATS check: the resume score and the suggestions in the ATS report are calculated on your device.
  • On-device AI: on supported devices some writing suggestions are generated by Apple's on-device models (Apple Intelligence). This processing happens on your device and your text is not sent to us.
  • Exported files: PDFs and images you export or share are created on your device and sent only where you choose (for example Mail, Files or AirDrop).

The App asks for access to your photo library (to select or save images) and your camera (to scan a printed resume or take a photo) only when you use those features. You can change these permissions at any time in your device Settings. The App can also send local notifications (for example reminders) if you allow them; these are scheduled on your device.

3. AI Writing Features and Third-Party AI (Anthropic Claude)

CVisto Pro includes cloud AI writing features, such as writing a professional summary, improving bullet points, writing cover letters and emails, reviewing a full resume and cleaning up imported resumes. These features are powered by Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic PBC (a third-party AI service).

We ask before anything is sent

The first time you use a cloud AI feature, the App shows a permission prompt explaining that your resume text will be sent to Anthropic's Claude AI. Nothing is sent unless you tap "Continue". If you choose "Cancel", nothing is shared and you can keep using the rest of the App.

What is sent

Only the content needed for the feature you selected, for example the text of your resume (which may include your name, job history, education and skills), the job title or company you are applying to, the bullet points you want to improve, or the notes you typed. Your profile photo is never sent. Content is sent only when you tap an AI button; nothing is sent in the background.

How it is sent and handled

  • Your request travels over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection to our server function hosted on Supabase, which forwards it to Anthropic and returns the result to your device.
  • We do not store the text you send or the text the AI returns. Only the usage record described in section 2 is kept.
  • Anthropic processes the content under its commercial API terms. Under those terms, Anthropic does not use data received through its API to train its models, and it keeps such data only for a limited period (for example for safety and abuse monitoring), as described in Anthropic's Privacy Policy and Commercial Terms.

Withdrawing permission

You can withdraw your permission at any time in Settings → "Send resume text to AI" inside the App. After you turn it off, no further content is sent to the AI service. If you use an AI feature again later, the App will ask for your permission again.

About AI output

AI-generated text can be inaccurate or incomplete. Always review it before using it in a resume or application. See our Terms of Use for details.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

CVisto uses a small number of service providers that are strictly necessary for the App to work:

  • Apple (App Store / In-App Purchase): processes all payments. We never receive your payment card or billing details. Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: validates purchases and manages subscription status using the anonymous user ID. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Supabase, Inc.: hosts our database (anonymous user ID, technical and subscription information, AI usage records) and the server function that connects to the AI service. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic PBC: provides the Claude AI model used for cloud AI writing features, only with your permission (see section 3). Anthropic Privacy Policy

These providers process data only on our behalf and for the purposes described in this policy. Each of them is bound by its own privacy and security commitments, which provide the same or equal protection of user data as described in this Privacy Policy and as required by Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.

CVisto does not use advertising SDKs, advertising networks, third-party analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. We never sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising, marketing profiles or data brokering.

5. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the providers' standard contractual clauses and data processing agreements.

6. Legal Bases (GDPR / KVKK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Türkiye, we process data on the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract: providing the App, validating subscriptions and counting AI credits.
  • Consent: sending your resume text to the third-party AI service. You can withdraw consent at any time as described in section 3.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and reliable and preventing abuse, using only minimal technical data.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it. You can delete any resume, cover letter or photo inside the App at any time. Deleting the App removes all locally stored data from your device. If your device backups (such as iCloud Backup) are enabled, a copy may also exist in your own backup, which is controlled by you and Apple.
  • Resume text sent to AI is not stored by us. Anthropic retains API data only for the limited period described in its policies.
  • Anonymous user ID, technical, subscription and AI usage records are kept while you use the App and for as long as needed for subscription management, accounting and legal obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

How to request deletion: because CVisto has no accounts, the data we hold is linked only to your anonymous user ID. To request deletion, email us at tugberktabak@yahoo.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include the User ID shown in the App's Settings screen. We will delete the related records within 30 days. Records that we are legally required to keep (for example for tax purposes) will be kept only as long as required. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription; subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing done before withdrawal;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the email address below. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. Data Security

All communication between the App and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS connections. API keys for the AI service are stored on the server and are never shipped in the App. Access to our database is restricted. Because most of your information stays on your device, its security also depends on your device; we recommend using a passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

10. Children's Privacy

CVisto is a tool for creating job application documents and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
